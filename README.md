## Chapter 10: The Targeted Civilian- Civic Life Knowledge Quiz
## Description 
The group project, Part_2.py, is a multiple-choice quiz based on Chapter 10, "The Targeted Civilian," from Weapons of Math Destruction. The questions focus on targeted political advertising, voter influence, data collection, and how algorithmic models are used in modern political campaigns.
In Chapter 10, O'Neil identifies targeted political advertising as a Weapon of Math Destruction (WMD) because it displays the three defining characteristics discussed throughout the book:
1.  Opacity – People do not usually know why they are being targeted.
2.  Scale – Messages can reach millions of voters with customized messages.
3.  Harm – Misleading messages can influence voter decisions.
## Contributions
Each member of the group contributed two multiple-choice questions for a total of eight questions. All members participated in researching Chapter 10, creating quiz content related to targeted political advertising and civic life, writing and testing the Python code for their own questions, and committing their work to GitHub.
